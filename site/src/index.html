Meet the printer

A pocket printer has moods. Here are the four you will see most.

Looking

Find printers scans over Bluetooth LE. There's nothing to pair first.

Printing

The job goes straight to the printer, row by row, at the pace it asks for.

Sent!

Sent means the printer got every byte. The paper has the final word.

Napping

Nothing in the queue. Quit any time: waiting jobs are still there after a restart.

Meet the app

Choose what to print on the left and watch the paper on the right. The preview is the exact dots the printer will heat.

Notes

Type a note and start a line with # to make it a heading. The app says how much paper it will use.

Pictures

Open a photo or a drawing, pick a tone, and see every dot before it prints. PDF pages work the same way.

Printers

Find printers lists the ones nearby. Check connection asks a printer how it's doing without using paper.

Your colours

Light or dark, five accent colours, and four app icons, the little printer included.

Questions

Is there a thermal printer app for PC that doesn't need a phone?

Yes. NuPrint runs on Windows, macOS and Linux and talks to the printer over Bluetooth straight from your computer. You don't install the printer's phone app, and you don't pair the printer first.

Which thermal printers does it work with?

The ZEITEL SC03h is tested on all three systems, and {{PRINTERS_COUNT}} more pocket Bluetooth models are offered as experimental. The printers page lists each one with the Bluetooth name it shows.

Is it free?

Yes. NuPrint is free and MIT licensed, with no account, no ads and no tracking.

Can I print PDFs and photos, not just text?

Yes. It prints notes with headings, pictures and single PDF pages, and shows the exact dots before anything uses paper.

Does it work on my system?

Windows 10 and 11 (Windows guide), macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon and Intel (Mac guide) and 64-bit Linux with BlueZ 5.80 or newer (Linux guide).
