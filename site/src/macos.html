A thermal printer app for Mac

NuPrint prints notes, pictures and PDF pages from a Mac to a pocket Bluetooth thermal printer. It is free, and it needs no phone app, no pairing and no printer driver.

For NuPrint {{TAG}} · macOS 14 or later · supported printers

Install it

  • Apple silicon (M1 and later): NuPrint_{{VERSION}}_aarch64.dmg
  • Intel Macs: NuPrint_{{VERSION}}_x64.dmg

The app isn't notarized yet, because that needs a paid Apple developer account. The first time you open it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway for NuPrint.

Print in four steps

  1. Turn on the printer, load thermal paper and close any printer app on your phone.
  2. In NuPrint, open the printer button, choose Find printers… and press Save printer on yours. The first time, macOS asks whether NuPrint may use Bluetooth. Choose Allow.
  3. Write a note, or open a picture or one page of a PDF. The preview shows exactly what will print.
  4. Set darkness, copies and extra feed, then press Print.

If you refused the Bluetooth question, Settings → Printers has an Open Bluetooth settings button. Turn NuPrint on there and find printers again.

Which printers work

The ZEITEL SC03h is tested on macOS. More models are offered as experimental. See the full list of supported printers.
