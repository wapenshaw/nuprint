# Code of conduct

NuPrint follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be welcoming and respectful, criticise ideas rather than people, and assume good faith.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/wapenshaw/nuprint/security/advisories/new), or by contacting @wapenshaw directly. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so.
